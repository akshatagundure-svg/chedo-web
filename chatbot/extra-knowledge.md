## How to enroll at Chedo Tech
Choose a course, then contact the team on WhatsApp or phone (+91 87931 00815) or visit the center in Kondhwa Budruk, Pune. The team will guide you, share the details for your course and help you pick a batch, and then you enroll and start learning.

## Mode of classes
All Chedo Tech courses are offline, in-person classroom courses at the Pune center. Classes are practical and project-based, with personal guidance from mentors. Classroom batch sizes are kept small so every student gets individual attention.

## Fees
Course fees are not published on the website. For the current fees of any course, message Chedo Tech on WhatsApp or call +91 87931 00815.
