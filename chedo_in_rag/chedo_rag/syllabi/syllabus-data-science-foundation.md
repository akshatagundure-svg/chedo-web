## About This Course

This is the beginner-friendly entry point into data science. You learn to think logically and solve problems in Python, then work with real data using Pandas and NumPy, build charts and basic dashboards, and understand simple statistics.
Alongside the technical track, you build practical creative AI skills — AI presentations, posters and flyers, animated videos, content creation and social media design — and finish with a real-world dataset project plus your own creative project. It is the perfect foundation before Machine Learning and AI.
